Speech test

Read each Bulgarian line aloud, as you would say it. The test measures whether this browser understands your Bulgarian well enough for a Speak tab (D-033). Say the line as written; if you misread it, press Retry.

30 sentences you have practised, then 10 single words. About ten minutes.

With Chrome, what you say is sent to Google's speech service, as with voice typing on Android. The page keeps no audio.

Laptop: Space to speak, Enter for next.
