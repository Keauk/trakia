Scheduling (FSRS)

The recall probability the scheduler aims for at review time. 0.90 is the FSRS default; above it the number of reviews per remembered sentence rises steeply, below it you see sentences less often but forget more. Changing it only affects future scheduling.

Each card has a stability (days until recall drops to 90 %) and a difficulty, updated from your own answers with the FSRS-6 algorithm Anki uses. New and lapsed cards go through short steps first: 10 min, 1 h, then days. Correct = Good, almost = Hard (or Good / Again by the rule below), wrong = Again.

Session

Mixed interleaves ranks and topics within a session, which is harder in the moment but retains better. Consecutive cards never share a word, and reviews are spread between new cards in both modes.

Answer checking

10 % ≈ one typo per ten characters still counts as almost. 0 = only exact matches are correct (always 1 char tolerance).
Staged: on a young card (still in the learning steps, or stability under a day) a near miss counts as correct, up to the number of free passes below (typos and word-order misses counted separately). On an established card (stability 1–7 days) it counts as Hard. On a mature card (stability 7 days or more) a clean answer is required, otherwise the card lapses. The verdict always still says "almost" and shows the exact diff.
