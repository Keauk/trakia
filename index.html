      Import sentences

      JSON (array of objects), CSV/TSV or Excel (.xlsx). Columns: bg, nl required; optional nl_alt, bg_alt, en_alt (separate alternatives with |), en, lemmas (comma-separated dictionary forms, used to compute the rank), tags, id. Rows without lemmas get an estimated rank (≈).

      Scheduling (FSRS)

      The recall probability the scheduler aims for at review time. 0.90 is the FSRS default; above it the number of reviews per remembered sentence rises steeply, below it you see sentences less often but forget more. Changing it only affects future scheduling.

      Each card has a stability (days until recall drops to 90 %) and a difficulty, updated from your own answers with the FSRS-6 algorithm Anki uses. A new card answered correctly goes straight to days; a missed or lapsed card comes back after 10 min. Correct = Good, almost = Hard (or Good / Again by the rule below), wrong = Again.

      Session

      Progress is kept per language, so you can switch at any time. Mixed assigns every sentence to Dutch or English once and keeps that assignment.
      Cards you answer in Dutch or English and cards you answer in Bulgarian come in blocks of this many, so you switch between the Latin and the Cyrillic keyboard less often. To keep the blocks full, up to one block minus one of the least urgent reviews waits for the next session; new sentences are never left out.
      Mixed interleaves ranks and topics within a session, which is harder in the moment but retains better. Consecutive cards never share a word, and reviews are spread between new cards in both modes.
      Every card in a session is a different sentence; a wrong answer comes back once at the end, on top of this number. A sentence you answered in the last 4 hours waits for a later session, so sessions in a row show new sentences.
      Reviews come first. While more reviews are due than fit in a session, it holds 2 new cards (1 in a session under 8 cards); once your reviews are done, new cards fill the free places, up to this number. The more you practise, the more new sentences you get, and reviews never pile up behind new ones.

      Answer checking

      10 % ≈ one typo per ten characters still counts as almost. 0 = only exact matches are correct (always 1 char tolerance).
      Staged: on a young card (its first three answers, still in a learning step, or stability under a day) a near miss counts as correct, up to the number of free passes below (typos and word-order misses counted separately). On an established card (stability 1–7 days) it counts as Hard. On a mature card (stability 7 days or more) a clean answer is required, otherwise the card lapses. The verdict always still says "almost" and shows the exact diff.
      A wrong word form is an error, not a typo
      On: within the typo tolerance, typing another form of a word (Sunday → Sundays, книгата → книга), swapping two short words (in → on) or leaving out a whole word counts as wrong. Real typos, like swapped letters or a missing space, still count as almost.
      Bulgarian word order is free
      Same words in another order counts as almost.
      Strict punctuation
      Off: punctuation and quotes are ignored.
      Strict ѝ
      Off: ѝ (her) and и are treated the same.
      Ignore Dutch accents
      één = een, café = cafe.

      Sound

      Sound on
      Off when you have no headphones: nothing plays, and the speaker buttons hide. Also the speaker in the top bar. Per device.
      Play by itself
      A Bulgarian prompt plays when the card appears; the Bulgarian answer plays when it is shown.
      Hearing a word from more than one speaker helps you recognise it. With both, each card picks a voice and every replay switches to the other. Stories always use Kalina, and Borislav for the men.
      Dictation
      Once you read a sentence well (three weeks between reviews) and have typed it in Bulgarian once, you hear it instead and type what you hear. With the sound off you get the text again.
      Clips are kept as they are played. Download all of them (about 200 MB, best on Wi-Fi) to hear everything offline.

      Wallpaper

      A short loop of a few megabytes works best. A chosen file stays on this device and takes precedence over the link. Film clips are copyrighted: use a clip you own or a free stock loop.
      The wallpaper pauses while the app is hidden, and is not shown when your system asks for reduced motion.

      Sync between devices

      Keeps your progress, settings, accepted answers and imported sentences the same on all your devices, through a secret gist on your GitHub account. Theme, wallpaper and keyboard settings stay per device.

      On github.com: Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens → Generate. Under Account permissions give only Gists: Read and write. Paste the same token on every device. It is kept in this browser only, never in exports or the gist.

      Syncs when the app opens, after every session, and when you switch away from it or back. On the first sync the progress of both sides is combined; for a sentence practised on both, the most recent answer counts.
