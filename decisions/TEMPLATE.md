# D-NNN: Title as a verb phrase, saying what changes for the learner

Status: proposed · Created: YYYY-MM-DD · Area: part of the app touched (`file`: `function`, …)

## Context
Why now. The current behaviour, what is wrong with it, and the evidence (measurements, research,
the learner's own report). Written so that a reader who has not used the app understands the problem.

## Proposal
What changes, as the learner will experience it. Numbered or bulleted. Name settings and their
defaults. Say what is explicitly not proposed.

## Design notes
How, tied to the current code: data fields, functions, migrations, tests, UI placement.

## Acceptance criteria
Observable checks that show the work is done: tests to add, behaviour on the phone, numbers to reach.

## Effort and risks
Rough size (lines, evenings), dependencies on other records, what could go wrong and the mitigation.

## As built
"Not started." until work begins. Then bullets: what shipped, and where it differs from the
proposal. Optional subsections, in this order:

### Decisions taken
Choices made during the work that the proposal left open or that changed it.

### Measurements
Numbers gathered during the work (pool sizes, coverage, timings), with the date.

## Open questions
Bullets. When a question is answered, strike it through and add the answer on the same bullet:
`- ~~Question?~~ Answer.` Write "None." if there are none.
